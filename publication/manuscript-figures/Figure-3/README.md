# Figure 3 | Financial vulnerability and feedback-dependent coupling

[SVG](Figure-3.svg) · [PDF](Figure-3.pdf) · [PNG](Figure-3.png) · [Scatter panel](adjusted-scatter.png) · [Slope and robust CI](plotted-values.tsv) · [Signed t map](FEVS_at_mean_PM25_domain_by_valence_z3p1_negative_signed-t.nii.gz) · [Mask](FEVS_at_mean_PM25_domain_by_valence_z3p1_negative_mask.nii.gz)

**Caption.** At mean PM₂.₅, higher financial vulnerability was associated with a smaller social relative to monetary win–loss difference in ventral striatal coupling. **A:** Left frontal target of the ventral striatal seed (44 voxels; peak MNI −20.8, 57.6, 11.2 mm; within-map cluster-FWE p = .0303 at Z-equivalent CDT 3.1). **B:** Each dot represents one of the 97 participants. The horizontal axis shows observed FEVS score; the vertical axis shows model-adjusted coupling in that target for (social win − social loss) − (monetary win − monetary loss). The line is the fitted association at mean PM₂.₅; shading is its pointwise 95% OLS confidence band, not a prediction interval. The fitted slope was −0.110 COPE units per FEVS point (robust 95% HC3 CI −0.155 to −0.066). This plot describes a cluster selected in these same participants; no corrected whole-brain cluster survived adding perceived support.

**Artwork and provenance.** Panel B is the complete middle panel of the [validated scatter diagnostic](../../../results/review/fevs-vs-v1/fevs-vs-cluster-1-scatter.png), not the raw-values or influence panel. The [model record](../../../results/review/fevs-vs-v1/summary.json) identifies the same cluster, cohort, FEVS coefficient and contrast. Each adjusted value is the fitted FEVS contribution plus intercept and that participant's residual from the original model (PM₂.₅, FEVS × PM₂.₅, age, gender, scanner era, and between-task FD/tSNR differences). The original plotting area and numeric ticks are a raster layer; no points were digitized, jittered, omitted or refit.
