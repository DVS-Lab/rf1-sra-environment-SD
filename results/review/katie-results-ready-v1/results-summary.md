# Katie: Results-ready evidence, November 7, 2026

Frozen N=97; exploratory and not preregistered. This is an evidence report, not a draft manuscript Results section.

## 1. Environmental stressors, social support, and financial vulnerability

The corrected PM2.5 × MSPSS interaction predicts FEVS: b=-0.989, HC3 95% CI [-1.505, -0.473], Holm p=.000509 across two primary tests. It persists with additive ADI, ECog, or both. At observed support percentiles 16/50/84 (3.417/5.833/6.917), the conditional PM2.5 slopes are 2.369/−0.020/−1.091. None of the other seven moderation tests survives its prespecified within-family Holm correction. OAFEM remains a distinct secondary financial measure. Marginal correlations involving support were recomputed with the corrected screener means, without significance testing.

## 2. Financial vulnerability and neural reward processing

The original negative FEVS coefficient at mean PM2.5 in the domain-by-valence VS-PPI model passes at CDT3.1 (44 voxels, p=.0303; frontal peak −20.8,57.6,11.2). Earlier additive ADI/ECog checks pass, but **adding corrected MSPSS does not**: negative-direction map minimum p=.0751 at CDT3.1 and .1021 at CDT2.6; no corrected clusters in either direction. This limits any claim that the original finding is robust to perceived support. No surviving adjusted location can be reported; aggregate outputs do not establish persistence of the unthresholded frontal peak or fixed-ROI coefficient after this adjustment.

Within the original selected 44-voxel region, original-model component slopes are social b=-0.043 (HC3 [-0.083, -0.003]), nonsocial b=0.067 ([0.039, 0.096]), and their difference b=-0.110 ([-0.155, -0.066]). These describe relative task-modulated coupling and are not independent confirmation or evidence of causality. Social-winning activation remains secondary: it loses corrected significance with ECog in the standalone model and substantially overlaps atlas-labeled white matter.

## 3. Environmental stressors and neural reward processing

Corrected ADI × MSPSS predicts social-minus-nonsocial winning activation (87 voxels; p=.0255; peak 54.8,−31.5,8.2). The selected-cluster ADI slope rises from negative at lower support to positive at higher support. All single-case-omission interaction estimates remain positive (1.160 to 1.335); this is descriptive, does not retest whole-brain correction, and does not establish protective buffering. The region is chiefly posterior temporal cortex in the atlas, with 23% white-matter labels and a white-matter-labeled peak in the subcortical atlas. ADI × ECog and PM2.5 × ECog findings concern VS connectivity, with influence limitations; their activation counterparts do not pass. Corrected PM2.5 × MSPSS and FEVS × MSPSS neural models have no surviving coefficients at either threshold.

## Reuse, new work, and remaining limitations

Original maps/masks/order/covariates and the cohort were retained. Corrected support replaces exactly 384 historical maps within the 1,328-map inventory; 71 maps have clusters, versus 69 historically. These are overlapping mapwise results, not independent findings; no global correction across exploratory models was performed.
